## Recommended Classical example

> 夏，歸粟於蔡，以周亟，矜無資。
>
> In summer, [Lu] sent grain to Cai, to relieve urgent need and show compassion for those without supplies.

Source: *Zuozhuan*, Duke Ding, year 5; *Chunqiu Zuozhuan zhengyi*, juan 55. [Text and commentary](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/春秋左傳正義/卷55).

Alignment: 夏 / in summer; 歸粟於蔡 / sent grain to Cai; 以 / to; 周亟 / relieve urgent need; 矜無資 / show compassion for those without supplies. Lu is supplied from the annalistic context, not written in this sentence. 亟 names the pressing need, as the object of 周; it does not modify the speed of sending grain. Du Yu explicitly glosses 亟，急也 and explains Cai's hunger following its siege by Chu. This is the strongest early candidate. 于/於 varies between textual witnesses; do not silently call that a substantive difference.

## Recommended Literary examples

### 楊掞's offer to relieve Anfeng

> 事亟矣，掞請行。
>
> The situation is urgent; I ask to go.

Source: *Songshi*, juan 412, liezhuan 171, biography of 楊掞. The preceding words are 逾年，安豐被兵，掞慨然曰; immediately afterward, 乃以奇策解圍. Anfeng is under attack and he offers to go, subsequently lifting the siege by an unexpected stratagem. 事 is the subject, 亟 the predicate, 矣 marks the current state; 掞 is the speaker's personal name used in first-person self-reference. Reading it “frequently” or “swiftly” cannot account for this syntax.

Edition check: Kanripo `KR2a0032`, `BASEEDITION WYG` (Wenyuan ge Siku quanshu transcription), juan 412, leaf **23b**, file lines **421–422**. The target wording agrees with the punctuated Wikisource text. This checks an edition-identified transcription, **not a freshly inspected facsimile**.

### Border emergency under Wu Yuan

> 邊事甚亟。
>
> Affairs on the frontier were very urgent.

Source: same juan, biography of 王登. In context: 及吳淵爲制置使，邊事甚亟，因憶弟潛盛言王登才略，具書幣招之. Wu Yuan summons Wang Deng because frontier affairs are pressing. 甚 modifies the predicate 亟, not a verb of movement or repeated action.

Edition check: same WYG transcription, leaf **22a**, file lines **392–393**; printed transcription graph 邉 is normalized to 邊 above. The same passage subsequently contrasts 淵慨然曰：「事亟矣，奈何？」 (“The situation is urgent; what are we to do?”) with 登曰：「亟呼諸將共議。」 (“Quickly summon the generals to deliberate together.”). The first 亟 describes circumstances; the second requests prompt action.

- [Edition-identified text, leaf 22a](https://github.com/kanripo/KR2a0032/blob/master/KR2a0032_412.txt#L391)
- [Punctuated context, 王登](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/宋史/卷412#王登)

The events are Southern Song; the *Songshi* is a Yuan compilation. These are Literary Chinese evidence, not pre-Qin attestations.

## Existing candidate: use with an interpretive qualification

> 其虛其邪，既亟只且。
>
> Can we still take our ease and delay? It is already urgent!

Source: *Shijing*, Beifeng, “Beifeng” 北風. This is a close contextual translation **following Zhu Xi**, not a neutral rendering accepted by every commentary. The implicit “it” is the situation; “danger” would be an explanatory addition.

Zhu Xi's *Shi jizhuan*, juan 2, glosses 虛 as 寬貌, 邪 as 徐緩, 亟 as 急, and 只且 as 語助辭; he explains 是尚可以寬徐乎 and 彼其禍亂之迫已甚而去不可不速矣. Thus the danger is pressing and departure cannot be delayed. [Siku transcription of Zhu's commentary](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/詩集傳_(四庫全書本)/卷2).

However, the Mao–Zheng tradition reads the contrast as formerly relaxed people becoming harsh/exacting in conduct (急刻之行); it does not simply deliver the same “already urgent situation” reading. See [*Mao Shi zhengyi*, juan 2](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/毛詩正義/卷二). The existing dossier translation should therefore be accompanied by its chosen interpretive tradition. Prefer the Zuozhuan and Songshi examples when an unambiguous teaching example is required.

## Selection

Use **事亟矣，掞請行** for the shortest transparent sentence, and **夏，歸粟於蔡，以周亟，矜無資** for early Classical evidence. **邊事甚亟** is an even shorter excerpt illustrating predicative 亟. Keep **既亟只且** as an existing qualified candidate. Exclude 亟其乘屋, 亟入關 and 亟去其位 from a strict urgent/pressing set: urgent contexts can motivate those instructions, but the word itself can be rendered “quickly/without delay.”
