# 亟: primary-text checks for urgency

Checked 2026-11-18. This note records source verification only; the accompanying consolidated report handles the user's dossier audit. Punctuation is editorial. The sources below are digital transcriptions, not independently collated printed editions or personally inspected facsimiles.

## Strong pre-imperial candidate: relief of urgent distress

> 夏，歸粟於蔡，以周亟，矜無資。

“In summer, [Lu] sent grain to Cai, to relieve its urgent distress and show compassion for its lack of resources.”

Source: *Zuo zhuan*, Duke Ding, year 5 (505 BCE in conventional chronology), received in *Chunqiu Zuozhuan zhengyi* 卷55. [Text, Du Yu's notes, Kong Yingda's subcommentary, and pronunciation gloss](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/春秋左傳正義/卷55).

The 經 note supplies the concrete context: 蔡為楚所圍，饑乏，故魯歸之粟 (“Cai had been besieged by Chu and suffered hunger and shortage, so Lu sent it grain”). The 傳 note explicitly reads 亟，急也, and the 音義 specifies 紀力反. Here 亟 is the object of 周, denoting the urgent need/distress relieved; it cannot mean “frequently” or simply describe the speed of sending grain. The parallel 矜無資 reinforces material distress.

Verification level: received original wording plus traditional gloss and narrative context checked in the complete digital chapter. Wikisource labels the work and commentarial layers but this page does not establish a specific printed base edition. Do not call it “checked against the Ruan Yuan edition” without a separate collation.

## Existing dossier candidate: 北風, qualified by interpretive tradition

> 其虛其邪？既亟只且！

Following Zhu Xi: “Can we still linger at ease? It is already urgent!” The subject of “urgent” is supplied from the implied situation. 只且 are final particles, not separately translated lexical content.

Source: *Shijing*, 邶風・北風, refrain in all three stanzas. The immediate context asks one's sympathetic companion to depart together: 惠而好我，攜手同行. [Zhu Xi, *Shijing jizhuan*, juan 2, digital transcription labelled 四庫全書本](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/詩集傳_(四庫全書本)/卷2).

The displayed heading identifies 欽定四庫全書、詩經集傳卷二、宋朱子撰. Zhu glosses 虚寛貌、邪一作徐緩也、亟急也、只且語助辭. His full relevant explanation is: 且曰是尚可以寛徐乎彼其禍亂之迫已甚而去不可不速矣. This supports pressing danger rather than simple quickness.

However, the earlier commentary interprets the same words differently. [*Mao Shi zhengyi*, juan 2, 北風](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/毛詩正義/卷二) has 毛傳 亟，急也, but Zheng Xuan explains formerly lenient officials now behaving 急刻, and Kong Yingda treats 既 as 盡. Under that interpretation the line concerns harsh/exacting conduct, not “the situation is already urgent.” Therefore cite the urgency translation explicitly as following Zhu Xi, and do not use Mao's bare 急 gloss to claim universal agreement.

Verification level: full original stanzas and both interpretive contexts checked in digital transcriptions. The Siku edition label was verified; a particular facsimile leaf was not visually checked.

## Tempting candidate with an edition-sensitive character

The phrase 天子之亟務也 is attributed to Sima Xiangru's 難蜀父老 and would illustrate “the Son of Heaven's urgent duty.” However, the [standalone Wikisource text](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/難蜀父老), linked there to *Wen xuan* juan 44, currently reads 天子之急務也. Search results point to witnesses with 亟, but those facsimiles were not successfully retrieved. Do not promote this candidate without checking an identified witness actually printing 亟.

## Scope and limitations

- These findings do not purport to collect every historical occurrence.
- No dossier files were edited.
- The strongest lexical distinction is syntactic: nominal 亟 after 周 is secure, while 亟 before an action often remains ordinary “quickly / without delay” even in an emergency.
- Facsimile search-result snippets alone were not treated as verified images.
