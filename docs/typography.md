# Typography

Reference for the design follow-ups labelled `design` in the issue tracker.

- Font loading and all visual styles are in `apps/web/app/styles.css`.
- The sizes below are the Figma design's, drawn for a 1512 px frame. Above 640 px wide with a mouse or trackpad, everything under the topbar renders at 75% (`zoom` on `.lesson-shell`), because that is the size that reads comfortably; a quotation, in a lesson or a dictionary page, undoes the zoom, so its sizes are real pixels. The topbar, phones and touch screens are not scaled.
- Lesson content is in `apps/web/app/content/lessons/001.mdx`.
- MDX presentation components are in `apps/web/app/components/lesson.tsx`.
- Original Chinese quote paragraphs are marked `lang="zh-Hant"`. They use LXGW WenKai Mono TC: in a lesson at weight 400, 28 px; in a dictionary page's etymology at weight 300, smaller.
- Chinese embedded in translations or Latin prose falls back to WenKai at the weight of its line. In lesson prose it is wrapped in `<span lang="zh-Hant">` at build time by `apps/web/app/mdx/rehype-han-runs.ts`; text that components get in a prop, such as a lesson subtitle, is wrapped the same way by `HanMarkedText`. Authors type plain characters (see `docs/lesson-authoring.md`).
- The topbar's tabs use Ancizar Serif at 18 px: weight 400, and 600 in red on the current tab.
- English lesson prose and translations use Source Serif 4: in a lesson, its text cut at every size except the lesson number; prose 24 px, translations 16 px italic, a quotation's provenance 13 px at 40% opacity.
- A quotation's lit mapping (the hover or touch highlight) is red. English also goes to weight 600, still italic. Chinese keeps its weight: LXGW WenKai's bold barely shows at this size, and lighting a character would download its bold file, showing a fallback font until it arrived.
- The seals — the site's 文 mark, the 課 "lesson" seal beside a lesson's number, and the A–J section markers — are drawn by `apps/web/app/lib/seal.ts` as stones printed in seal paste `#d9383c` (deeper than the site's red) through an SVG filter, and baked by `scripts/bake-seals.ts` into `public/seals/*.png` at 3×, so every browser shows the same print; the page shows the images (`components/seal.tsx`), sized in `styles.css`. 課 is a traced outline of HanWang WCL-02 (王漢宗特明體一標準, GPL v2: the outline is used as a drawing, the font is not shipped) and 文 one of Ma Shan Zheng (OFL). The markers' letters are Source Serif 4 at weight 600; the lesson number is Source Serif 4 at weight 900, in its display cut.
- Large section-head characters (A, B, C, and so on) use Noto Serif TC at weight 500, 144 px, and a dictionary page's headword uses it at weight 400, smaller. In a lesson the character's reading is Source Serif 4 at 40 px. A dictionary headword's readings are Source Serif 4, upright, as the Figma design ("Dictionary Entry Views") sets them; a sense's examples write their Chinese in WenKai.
- Form-study glyphs and standalone character-focus displays use TW-Kai (全字庫正楷體) at 128 px, the nearest open font to the Figma design's BiauKai. The site ships the whole font as `public/fonts/tw-kai.woff2` (15 MB); it is to be cut down to the displayed characters before launch (`docs/lesson-authoring.md`).
