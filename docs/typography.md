# Typography

Reference for the design follow-ups labelled `design` in the issue tracker.

- Font loading and all visual styles are in `apps/web/app/styles.css`.
- Lesson content is in `apps/web/app/content/lessons/001.mdx`.
- MDX presentation components are in `apps/web/app/components/lesson.tsx`.
- Original Chinese quote paragraphs are marked `lang="zh-Hant"`. They use LXGW WenKai Mono TC: in a lesson at weight 400, 28 px; in a dictionary page's etymology at weight 300, smaller.
- Chinese embedded in translations or Latin prose falls back to WenKai at weight 400. In lesson prose it is wrapped in `<span lang="zh-Hant">` at build time by `apps/web/app/mdx/rehype-han-runs.ts`; text that components get in a prop, such as a lesson subtitle, is wrapped the same way by `HanMarkedText`. Authors type plain characters (see `docs/lesson-authoring.md`).
- The topbar's tabs use Ancizar Serif at 18 px: weight 400, and 600 in red on the current tab.
- English lesson prose and translations use Source Serif 4, in its text cut at every size: prose 24 px, translations 16 px italic, a quotation's provenance 13 px at 40% opacity.
- A quotation's lit mapping (the hover or touch highlight) is red and a bit heavier: Chinese at weight 700, English at 600, still italic.
- The seals — the site's 文 mark, the 課 "lesson" seal beside a lesson's number, and the A–J section markers — are inline SVG stones generated by `apps/web/app/components/seal.tsx`; their sizes are in `styles.css`. 課 is a traced outline of HanWang WCL-02 (王漢宗特明體一標準, GPL v2: the outline is used as a drawing, the font is not shipped) and 文 one of Ma Shan Zheng (OFL). The markers' letters are Source Serif 4 at weight 600; the lesson number is Source Serif 4 at weight 900, in its display cut.
- Large section-head characters (A, B, C, and so on) use Noto Serif TC at weight 500, 144 px, and a dictionary page's headword uses it at weight 400, smaller. In a lesson the character's reading is Source Serif 4 at 40 px. A dictionary headword's readings are Source Serif 4, upright, as the Figma design ("Dictionary Entry Views") sets them; a sense's examples write their Chinese in WenKai.
- Form-study glyphs and standalone character-focus displays use WenKai Mono TC Bold at 128 px, the nearest web font to the Figma design's BiauKai.
