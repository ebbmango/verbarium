The 13th radical of the *Kangxi Zidian* (康熙字典). As a component it can picture a cloud: in 雨, the *Shuowen Jiezi* (說文解字) reads it so.

<Entry>
  <Reading pinyin="jiōng" />

  <Sense gloss="distant borderlands">
    The farthest land from a city: beyond its outskirts, its open country and its forests, as the *Shuowen Jiezi* lays them out.
