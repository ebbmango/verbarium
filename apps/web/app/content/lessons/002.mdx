<CharDisplay character="二" label="A" reading="èr" />

Two.

The number of the earth, \
because it makes a pair with heaven.

{/* L002A-Q01 · Wieger: 地之數也。 · found/verbatim (received Shuowen, entry 二; also the head of Zhu's entry 二) */}
<LegacyQuote source="Shuowen Jiezi" sourceHref="https://ctext.org/shuo-wen-jie-zi/er-bu3#:~:text=%E4%BA%8C%EF%BC%9A%E5%9C%B0%E4%B9%8B%E6%95%B8%E4%B9%9F">
  地之數也。从偶一。

  The number of Earth. \
  Formed from a pair of ones.
</LegacyQuote>

The number of the two principles.

{/* L002A-Q02 · Wieger: 陰陽之數也。 · found/verbatim (Zhu, entry 二, quoting Shuoyuan 辨物; Wieger drops 二者) */}
<LegacyQuote source="Shuoyuan" sourceHref="https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=gb&node=22109&show=parallel#:~:text=%E4%BA%8C%E8%80%85%E9%99%B0%E9%99%BD%E4%B9%8B%E6%95%B8%E4%B9%9F">
  二者陰陽之數也。

  Two, the number of Yin and Yang.
</LegacyQuote>

<CharDisplay character="仁" label="B" reading="rén" />

Benevolence.

<CharacterForms character="仁" description="Old and new forms of the character" historical="sinica-10-small-seal-仁" />

The fundamental virtue of Confucianism,\
ordinarily translated as *humaneness.*

{/* L002B-Q01 · Wieger: 親也。从人、从二、會意。相親謂之仁。 · found/paraphrase: opening = received Shuowen, entry 仁 (Zhu adds 會意) */}
<LegacyQuote source="Shuowen Jiezi" sourceHref="https://ctext.org/shuo-wen-jie-zi/ren-bu1#:~:text=%E4%BB%81%EF%BC%9A%E8%A6%AA%E4%B9%9F%E3%80%82%E4%BB%8E%E4%BA%BA%E4%BB%8E%E4%BA%8C%E3%80%82">
  親也。从人从二。

  Kinship. Formed from 人 and from 二.
</LegacyQuote>

To love each other.

The benevolence that must link each\
亻 man with 二 his neighbour.

{/* L002B-Q02 · Wieger's tail 相親謂之仁 = Liji 經解, as Zhu quotes it in entry 仁 (Wieger drops 上下) */}
<LegacyQuote source="Liji" sourceHref="https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=gb&node=61476&show=parallel#:~:text=%E4%B8%8A%E4%B8%8B%E7%9B%B8%E8%A6%AA%EF%BC%8C%E8%AC%82%E4%B9%8B%E4%BB%81">
  上下相親，謂之仁。

  High and low hold one another dear,\
  this is called love.
</LegacyQuote>

<CharDisplay character="佞" label="C" reading="nìng" />

Coaxing.

<CharacterForms character="佞" description="Old and new forms of the character" historical="sinica-01-small-seal-佞" />

{/* L002C-Q01 · Wieger: 巧諂也。 · found/paraphrase (3rd, 4th and 7th characters of Xu Kai's sentence) */}
<LegacyQuote source="Shuowen Jiezi Xizhuan" sourceHref="https://ctext.org/library.pl?if=en&file=77400&page=87">
  佞者巧諂高才也。

  The smooth-tongued is\
  artful in flattery, of great talent.
</LegacyQuote>

Flattery, the fellowship of women.

The authors of the Chinese characters have been hard on the daughters of Eve.

{/* L002C-Q02 · probable source of Wieger's English "the 仁 of 女 women"; a few lines after Q01 */}
<LegacyQuote source="Shuowen Jiezi Xizhuan" sourceHref="https://ctext.org/library.pl?if=en&file=77400&page=88">
  佞者女子之仁，\
  故于文女仁為佞。

  Fawning is a womanly kindliness;\
  thus, in writing, 女 and 仁 make 佞.
</LegacyQuote>

<Commentary>
  I think the etymology runs deeper here.

  Xu Kai himself says:

  {/* L002C-Q03 · Same passage as Q01, CText file 77400, p. 87, columns 7–8. */}
  <LegacyQuote source="Shuowen Jiezi Xizhuan" sourceHref="https://ctext.org/library.pl?if=en&file=77400&page=87">
    有小才不以正道近其君，\
    曰佞也。

    One who possesses some talent but uses improper means to gain closeness to his ruler is called 佞.
  </LegacyQuote>

  To attain someone's goodwill by flattery;

  This is tantamount to *seduction:*\
  the way a 女 woman might entice a man's 仁 affection.

</Commentary>

<Commentary>


{/* L002C-Q04 · Kong Yingda’s subcommentary on Duke Cheng, year 13, 寡人不佞 (“I am without talent”); not the Zuozhuan base text or Du Yu’s note. */}
<LegacyQuote source="Chunqiu Zuozhuan Zhengyi" sourceHref="https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/%E6%98%A5%E7%A7%8B%E5%B7%A6%E5%82%B3%E6%AD%A3%E7%BE%A9/%E5%8D%B727#成公十有三年傳">
  佞是口才捷利之名，\
  本非善惡之稱。

  佞 is the name for quick and sharp verbal talent;\

It also means *eloquence*,\

<CharDisplay character="亟" label="D" reading="jí" />

Activity, working up of faculties.\
Struggle for life.

<CharacterForms character="亟" description="Old and new forms of the character" historical="sinica-11-small-seal-亟" />

A 人 man who struggles,\
with his 口 mouth and his <InlineForm name="又-bigseal.svg" character="又" /> hand,\
between 二 heaven and earth,\
to gain his point.

{/* L002D-Q01 · Wieger: 从人、从口、从[hand]、會意。从二、天地也、指事。人生天地間、手口並作敏疾成事也。 · found/verbatim (Zhu, entry 亟; Wieger prints 二 once) */}
<LegacyQuote source="Shuowen Tongxun Dingsheng" sourceHref="https://archive.org/details/shuowentongxundi110zhuj/page/n852/mode/1up">
  人生天地間，手口並作，敏疾成事也。

  Man lives between Heaven and Earth;\
  hand and mouth working together,\
  he swiftly brings his affairs to completion.
</LegacyQuote>

<Commentary>

Urgency.

{/* L002D-Q02 · Songshi, juan 412, biography of Wang Deng; Wenyuan-ge Siku transcription, leaf 22a (邉 normalized to 邊). */}
<LegacyQuote source="Songshi" sourceHref="https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/宋史/卷412#王登">
  邊事甚亟。

  Affairs on the frontier were very urgent.
</LegacyQuote>

Wieger's hazy definition captures the outward appearance of urgent action.

</Commentary>
